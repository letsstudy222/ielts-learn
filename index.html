📖 Ngữ Pháp IELTS Reading

1. Present Simple vs Present Continuous

Giải thích: Present Simple dùng cho sự thật chung, thói quen. Present Continuous dùng cho hành động đang xảy ra.

✓ I work as an engineer (sự thật)

✓ I am working on a project (đang làm)

2. Relative Clauses (Mệnh đề quan hệ)

Giải thích: Dùng để cung cấp thông tin thêm về danh từ.

✓ The man who lives next door is a doctor

✓ The book which I read was interesting

3. Passive Voice (Câu bị động)

Giải thích: Diễn tả hành động được thực hiện cho chủ thể.

✓ The house was built in 1990

✓ English is spoken worldwide

📝 Bài Đọc Mẫu

Chủ Đề: Daily Life in Modern Cities

Modern cities have transformed dramatically over the past century. The rapid urbanization has brought both opportunities and challenges to millions of people worldwide. Today's cities are characterized by towering skyscrapers, complex transportation networks, and diverse populations. Despite these developments, urban living comes with significant challenges such as pollution, overcrowding, and increased living costs.

However, cities also offer numerous advantages. They provide better access to education, healthcare, and employment opportunities. Cultural diversity in cities creates vibrant communities where people from different backgrounds can interact and share experiences. Furthermore, cities are often centers of innovation and creativity.

Câu Hỏi:

1. Theo đoạn văn, thách thức chính của cuộc sống thành thị là gì?

2. Lợi ích nào được đề cập trong đoạn thứ hai?
